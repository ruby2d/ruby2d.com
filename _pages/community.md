Ruby 2D is built by a small but mighty community of folks who love making graphics with Ruby. Whether you're just getting started or have been hacking on 2D apps for a while, we'd love to have you around.

## Chat with us on Discord

Our [Discord server](https://chat.ruby2d.com) is the best place to ask questions, share what you're building, or just hang out. Drop by the **#support** channel if you need a hand.

## Contribute on GitHub

Ruby 2D is open source and lives [on GitHub](https://github.com/ruby2d/ruby2d). Found a bug? Have an idea? Let us know!
