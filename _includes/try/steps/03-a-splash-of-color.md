# A splash of color

Colors come in a few flavors, and they all go in the same `color:` spot. There are [named colors](/learn/colors) like `'orange'` and `'fuchsia'`, hex strings like `'#7f5af0'` for when you want something exact, and `'random'` for when you're feeling lucky.

The big square at the bottom is the fun one: give a square *four* colors, one per corner, and Ruby 2D blends them into a gradient. And `opacity:` makes any shape translucent, from `0` (invisible) to `1` (solid).

**Try this:** run this a few times and watch the random square reinvent itself. Then swap one of the gradient corners for `'black'` and see the mood change.
