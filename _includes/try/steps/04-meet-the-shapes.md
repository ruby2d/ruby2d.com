# Meet the shapes

Squares have friends. `Circle` takes a `radius`, `Rectangle` takes a `width` and `height`, `Triangle` takes three corners, and `Line` stretches between two points.

One thing to notice: a circle's `x` and `y` are its *center*, not a corner (a circle doesn't have corners to offer). And that two-color line? Same gradient trick you just learned, one color per end.

**Try this:** give the circle an outline with `stroke_width: 8, stroke_color: 'white'`. Then see if you can make the triangle point downward by moving its corners.
